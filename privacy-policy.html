Privacy Policy

Effective September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how The Mat Guy collects and uses information submitted through our lead-capture forms, including forms on Facebook and Instagram, our website, and email inquiries.

Information we collect

Name, email address, phone number, project location (city/state), project type, and timing of need, as voluntarily submitted when requesting information about our mat rental and construction-site mat services.

How we use it

To respond to inquiries, provide quotes, and follow up about our products and services; we do not sell or rent this information to third parties.

Where it is stored

Submissions are stored in our internal CRM (Pipedrive) and operations system, accessible only to our sales and operations staff.

Data retention

We retain lead information for as long as needed to service the inquiry and for reasonable business record-keeping thereafter.

Your choices

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, email bret@tribalwire.com.

Third-party platforms

When a lead form is submitted through Facebook or Instagram, Meta's own privacy policy also applies to that platform's handling of the submission.

Contact
